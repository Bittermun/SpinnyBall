How SpinnyBall Works

SpinnyBall uses spin-stabilized magnetic packets circulating along an orbital circumference to enable station-keeping in cislunar space. The system leverages momentum-flux anchoring and flux-pinned superconducting bearings for passive stiffness.

Physics Principles

  • Gyroscopic dynamics: I·ω̇ + ω×(I×ω) = τ
  • Flux-pinning: Jc(B,T) = Jc0·(1-T/Tc)^n·f(B)
  • Effective stiffness: k_eff = λu²g_gain + k_fp
  • Centrifugal stress: σ = m·ω²r/(4πr)

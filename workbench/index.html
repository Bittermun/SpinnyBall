ABOUT THIS LABORATORY

Observe the model.
Question the result.

These are deliberately small numerical experiments. They help isolate a physical principle before adding the complexity of a proposed machine.

How to read the checks

The energy chart plots (E − E₀ − external work) divided by a documented energy scale. The momentum check includes external impulse; the spin check uses the full angular momentum vector in the inertial frame. Maxima include every integration step, even when the plots show fewer samples.

The 0.1% indicator is a numerical diagnostic, not evidence of physical accuracy. Halve the integration step and compare the outcome. Energy conservation alone cannot prove that an orbit's phase or a model's assumptions are correct.

Reproduce an experiment

Save experiment includes the parameters, initial state, engine version, state samples, assumptions, source and diagnostics. Import recalculates from those parameters. CSV exports retained samples; the JSON carries their units and provenance. A pinned run is kept only in this tab; save it before closing.

What changed?

The earlier magnetic mass-stream and “digital twin” prototypes are retained in the repository archive. Their feasibility claims and simulated material readings are not validated by this laboratory. This release makes no claim about a working orbital anchor.
